Rise of Reliance Industries

From a small yarn trading business to a global conglomerate - The remarkable journey of India's most valuable company

1957

The Humble Beginnings

Dhirubhai H Ambani was a son of a school teacher from Junagadh, Gujarat. After his schooling he went to Aden, UK and returned in 1957 and started his yarn trading business named Majin in Mumbai majorly into importing yarn and exporting spices to Yemen.

1977

The Historic IPO

Reliance Textile Industries' IPO created history by ushering the equity cult in India. The issue was oversubscribed seven times, strengthening Reliance's growth ambitions. The IPO, which issued 2.8 million shares, was credited with engaging small investors in the stock market.

1985

Diversification Begins

With investors eager to own shares, Reliance expanded rapidly into sectors adjacent to its textile business, and in 1985 it became known as Reliance Industries Limited. After gaining the capacity to manufacture petrochemicals useful for synthetic textiles, it later began manufacturing plastics and, eventually, refining petroleum.

1991

Hazira Complex

In 1991 Reliance opened a massive industrial complex in Hazira, Gujarat state, propelling the corporation to the forefront of production in the global polyester market.

1996

Entering Oil Refining

Reliance entered the refining business with the setting up of a world-class refinery in Jamnagar. This marked a significant shift from textiles and petrochemicals into oil refining, which would become a major pillar of its business.

1998

International Recognition

15 June 1998 – Dhirubhai Ambani received the Dean's Medal by The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania for setting an outstanding example of leadership. Dhirubhai Ambani was the first Indian to receive the Dean's Medal.

2002

Leadership Transition

After Dhirubhai's death in 2002, his sons Mukesh and Anil assumed joint leadership of the Reliance companies. However, the brothers' feuding over control prompted their mother, Kokilaben, to split Reliance's assets via a noncompetition agreement.

2004

Global Recognition

Reliance emerges as the first and only private Indian organisation to be listed in the Fortune Global 500 ® list under the leadership of Mukesh D. Ambani as Chairman and Managing Director of Reliance Industries Limited.

2006

Retail Revolution

Reliance enters the retail business with Reliance Retail and quickly becomes the largest retailer by revenue in just eight years.

2007

Media Expansion

Reliance entered the media and entertainment sector by acquiring stakes in TV and media companies, making its presence felt in the growing Indian media space.

2010s

Digital Disruption

The company's growth continued, with investments in energy, petrochemicals, telecommunications, and retail. In 2016, Reliance launched Jio, a disruptive telecommunications company that revolutionized India's mobile internet market with affordable data plans and widespread 4G services.

2020s

Green Energy Pivot

Reliance made major moves toward sustainability and green energy. Chairman Mukesh Ambani announced plans to shift Reliance's focus toward renewable energy, aiming to build one of the world's largest green energy ecosystems.
